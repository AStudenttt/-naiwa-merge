# Milk Frog Merge
### 合成大奶蛙

**English** · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

A small browser game about dropping frogs, making bigger frogs, and running out of room.

**[Play now](https://astudenttt.github.io/milk-frog-merge/)** · [Report a bug](https://github.com/AStudenttt/milk-frog-merge/issues)

## Meet Milk Frog

Legend has it that the world used to be completely frog-free. Then one day, a loud laugh came from the sky, followed by something yellow. It landed belly-up, eyes pointing in different directions. Not quite a dragon. Not quite a frog. People called it Milk Frog and left it at that.

Milk Frogs don’t eat much or do much. Mostly, they laugh. But when two matching frogs bump into each other, they become one bigger frog. People thought this was a blessing. Then they ran out of space in the yard.

So they built a container and set out to merge the biggest Milk Frog ever seen.

This is that container. Good luck.

## Small frogs. Bigger problems.

Drop Milk Frogs into the container. When two of the same level touch, they merge into a larger one. Work your way through 11 levels, build your score, and keep the pile under control.

The frogs bounce, wobble, and make some questionable faces when things get crowded.

## How to play

- **Touch:** Drag to choose a position, then release to drop.
- **Keyboard:** Use the arrow keys to move and Space to drop.
- **Merge:** Match two frogs of the same level.
- **Stay below the line:** Frogs lingering at the warning line trigger a countdown.

Open the gallery to see the frog lineup—including their less dignified expressions.

## Play in your browser

No download required. Designed for desktop and touch screens.

The game interface is currently in Chinese.
